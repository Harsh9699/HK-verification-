Course Curriculum

From Zero to Your First Live AI-Built Website. No prior tech knowledge required.

Phase 1: Foundation — Understanding AI Day 1–3

Day 1

What is AI, Really?

We explain AI using everyday analogies — think of it as a very well-read friend. We clarify the difference between a normal app and an AI tool with a live interactive demo.

🛠️ Tools: ChatGPT (free), Google Gemini — demo only
Day 2

How Does AI Actually Work? (No Jargon)

Introduce the idea of "pattern recognition" without any math. We cover the different types of AI at a glance: chat-based, image-based, video-based, and voice-based.

💡 Concept-focused session
Day 3

First Hands-On Session

Every student creates their own free account. Practice asking simple, everyday questions to build comfort and completely remove the fear of "using AI".

🛠️ Tools: ChatGPT, Google Gemini (free accounts)

Phase 2: Basic Tool Usage Day 4–7

Day 4

Asking Good Questions (Prompting Basics)

Learn that clearer questions get better answers. We show side-by-side examples of a weak prompt vs. a strong prompt and how the output changes.

🛠️ Tools: ChatGPT
Day 5

AI for Studies & Work

Use AI to summarize notes, solve doubts from a PDF, or extract information from textbook pages — directly useful and relatable skills.

🛠️ Tools: NotebookLM (free)
Day 6

AI Image Generation

A fun, high-engagement session! Each student generates a cartoon version of themselves or a simple creative image from scratch.

🛠️ Tools: ChatGPT (image generation)
Day 7

AI Voice & Video Generation

Introduce voice generation, voice cloning, and short AI-generated video clips. This session usually has the strongest "wow factor"!

🛠️ Tools: ElevenLabs (voice), Google Flow (video)

Phase 3: Light-Advanced — Building Skills Day 8–12

Day 8

What Is a Website, Really?

A concept-only session explaining a webpage as a structure plus design — just like building a house: foundation, rooms, and decoration.

💡 Concept-focused session
Day 9

Generating a Landing Page with AI

Students prompt an AI tool to build a landing page for a sample business idea, then see it come to life instantly — the first major "I built something" moment.

🛠️ Tools: Claude, ChatGPT, Lovable / v0.dev
Day 10

Customizing the Landing Page

Combine everything learned so far — generate images and text with AI and add them seamlessly into your new landing page layout.

🛠️ Tools: Lovable / v0.dev, ChatGPT
Day 11

Adding a Chatbot to the Website

Embed a simple AI chatbot directly into your landing page — "now your page can talk back."

🛠️ Tools: Google AI Studio
Day 12

Going Live: Deployment

Deploy the landing page using free hosting so it is accessible on the internet — a genuine "the world can see this" milestone.

🛠️ Tools: Netlify or Vercel

Phase 4: Capstone & Wrap-Up Day 13–15

Day 13

Capstone Polish Day

Students finalize their own landing page project, choosing a personal brand or sample business idea, combining all tools learned across the course.

🛠️ Tools: All tools used so far
Day 14

Final Touches + AI Safety Awareness

Quick, important discussion on deepfakes, fake news, and using AI responsibly — essential knowledge for a beginner audience.

💡 Discussion session
Day 15

Presentation & Certification

Each student presents their own live, deployed landing page. Certificates are distributed and the course closes with guidance on continuing to learn AI independently.
